# Team invitations
- Team Owner/Admin can invite family, crew, or another Team Admin.
- Invite may originate from phone number or email.
- Invite expires after 7 days.
- Token is stored only as a SHA-256 hash.
- A dependent rider still needs no login/email/phone and is created directly by Team Owner/Admin.
- Team Admin is team-scoped only; accepting a Team Admin invite never grants PitBuzz system/race administration.
